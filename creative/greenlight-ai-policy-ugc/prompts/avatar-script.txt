GREENLIGHT EP01 "TWO COPILOTS" — AVATAR-TOOL SCRIPT
For HeyGen, Arcads, Creatify, Captions.ai — tools where you pick a stock UGC
presenter and paste a script. This is the fastest route to a real-looking person
with real audio, and the one that needs the least fixing afterwards.

AVATAR SELECTION — reject anything that looks like a spokesperson
  ✓ Filmed handheld, at arm's length, in a real room
  ✓ Ordinary clothes, no blazer, no lapel mic visible
  ✓ Mid-thirties, talks fast, moves while talking
  ✓ Office, kitchen or corridor background — not a studio, not a sofa set
  ✗ Any avatar in front of a plain wall or a bookshelf-with-plant
  ✗ Any avatar that opens with a wave or a smile-and-hold
  ✗ Any avatar labelled "spokesperson", "presenter", "corporate" or "luxury"

VOICE — British, female, mid-thirties, conversational. Speed 1.05-1.10x.
Do not use a "narration" or "documentary" voice preset. It should sound like
someone leaving a voice note, not reading a script.

SCRIPT — paste as-is. Ellipses are breath, not silence. Total ~30 seconds.

Two Copilots on your laptop. Only one's actually ours.
Same icon, same name... completely different rules.
Work runs inside our Microsoft tenant. That's the safe one.
Internal, confidential, client-confidential, restricted — personal data too. All fine in Work.
Web is the public internet. Nothing of ours goes in.
Three catches: privileged stuff stays out of Copilot entirely. A contract that bans AI beats your licence. And no recording externals without everyone's yes.
Either way, it still gets a human read before it ships.
Check the toggle before you type.

AFTER GENERATING
1. Burn in captions from ep01.srt (or let the tool auto-caption — check the
   hyphenated terms, they get mangled: "client-confidential", "Copilot").
2. Colour two words and nothing else: WORK green, WEB red.
3. Add the lane chip top-left and the "AI-generated presenter" mark bottom-right.
4. Cut the tool's outro card if it adds one.
