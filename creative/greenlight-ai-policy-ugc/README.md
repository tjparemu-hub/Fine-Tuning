# GREENLIGHT — Nteractive AI Policy, short-form video series

A UGC-style vertical video series that translates the **ISMS Policy: AI Governance and Usage**
(Owner: Paul Smith, Head of IT · published 7 July 2026) into short-form video staff will
actually watch.

**The name does double duty.** "Greenlight" is a film term and an approval term — every
episode answers one question: *is this greenlit?*

## Why UGC and not a corporate explainer

The policy's own words: *"This policy is not intended to limit innovation."* A polished
corporate video reads as a restriction notice. A colleague filming at their desk reads as
a heads-up. The second one gets watched to the end, and the end is where the CTA lives.
